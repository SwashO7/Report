1. PRODUCT PURPOSE

Suraksha Labs is a free cybersecurity learning and practical training platform operated by Shadow Security.

Its purpose is to take someone who may know little or nothing about cybersecurity and gradually develop their practical cybersecurity skills through:

Learning → Practice → Labs → Progression → Community → Cybersecurity competency

The eventual ecosystem will lead strong learners toward Suraksha VDP, but VDP-readiness examinations/certification are NOT part of this current implementation.

Do not implement the future VDP examination system now.

The current product must focus on making Suraksha Labs itself exceptional.

The central product philosophy:

Anyone should be able to start cybersecurity from zero, learn concepts in a practical way, immediately apply them, solve realistic security challenges, see measurable progress, and feel proud of their achievements.

2. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Build the product around these principles:

Beginner-friendly.
Practical rather than theory-heavy.
Learn concepts and solve challenges as separate experiences.
Progression from absolute beginner to advanced cybersecurity topics.
Local CTF-style labs for the initial infrastructure model.
Strong gamification without making the website look like a childish game.
Premium, calm, modern UX.
Strong identity for Suraksha Labs.
Extremely modular architecture.

Excellent performance.
Easy future expansion.
No unnecessary infrastructure cost.
No developer should need to modify application source code every time the team wants to add or modify educational content.

18. LAB SYSTEM

The initial lab infrastructure should be:

LOCAL CTF-STYLE LABS

Do NOT build a cloud container-per-user system for V1.

The backend validates the flag and awards the completion.

This dramatically reduces infrastructure cost.
